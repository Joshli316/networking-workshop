场景Situation 中文（说出来）Chinese EnglishEnglish
告诉朋友您在找工作Tell a friend you're job-hunting "想跟您说一下，我最近在找 ___ 方面的工作。如果您知道有公司在招人，能告诉我吗？谢谢！""I wanted to mention I'm looking for ___ work. If you hear of openings, please let me know. Thanks!" "I wanted to mention I'm looking for ___ work. Please let me know if you hear of anything. Thanks!"
问公司有没有空缺Ask if their company is hiring "您公司最近招人吗？有什么方面的工作？我对 ___ 比较感兴趣。""Is your company hiring? What kind of role? I'd love to work in ___." "Is your company hiring? I'd be interested in a ___ role."
请教信息访谈Request an informational interview "嗨 ___，我看到您在 ___ 做 ___。我也想进入这一行。可以请您喝 20 分钟咖啡，请教经验吗？""Hi ___, I saw you work at ___ as ___. I'm hoping to enter that field. LinkedIn 设置清单LinkedIn Setup Checklist
10 steps to a basic LinkedIn profile that recruiters can find10 步建立一个招聘官能找到的基础资料
步骤Step 操作What to Do 提示Tip
1. 下载 App1. Download App App Store 或 Play 商店 — 搜「LinkedIn」App Store / Play Store — search "LinkedIn" 免费Free
2. 切换中文2. Switch to Chinese 设置 → 账户 → 语言 → 简体中文Settings → Account → Language → 简体中文 界面变中文，方便操作Easier for first-time use
3. 上传照片3. Upload photo 正面头像，白色或浅色背景，微笑Front-facing, plain background, smile 不要用婚纱照、风景照、自拍No wedding photos, no scenery, no selfies
4. 写标题4. Headline 「Seeking [职位] roles in Los Angeles」"Seeking [Job Title] roles in Los Angeles" 用英语，简单清楚English, short, clear
5. 工作经历5. Work history 列出过去 10 年所有工作，简单英语List all jobs from last 10 years, simple English 就算是在中国的工作也要写Include jobs from overseas too
6. 教育经历6. Education 学校 + 学历 + 年份（英文学校名）School + degree + year (use English school name) 高中也算High school counts
7. 技能7. Skills 加 10 个技能：英语、中文、Excel、客服等Add 10 skills: English, Mandarin, Excel, customer service, etc. 中文也是技能"Mandarin" is a real skill
8. 加联系人8. Connect 先加家人、朋友、同事 — 目标 50 个Start with family, friends, coworkers — aim for 50 50+ 个之后雇主才能搜到您Recruiters can find you after 50+
9. 打开「Open to Work」9. Turn on "Open to Work" 个人资料 → 「打开求职状态」Profile → "Open to Work" → green circle 雇主看到您绿圈Recruiters see your green ring
10. 每天 10 分钟10. 10 min daily 点赞 5 个 post，评论 1 个Like 5 posts, comment on 1 活跃账号 = 雇主信任Active accounts get more views

实用英语短语Useful English Phrases
Quick phrases for networking events and job fairs招聘会、社区活动上能用的常用句
中文Chinese 英语（说出来）English (say this) 使用场景When to use
您好，我叫 ___。Hi, I'm ___. "Hi, my name is ___." 初次见面First meeting
您是做什么工作的？What do you do? "What kind of work do you do?" 破冰提问Icebreaker question
我在找 ___ 方面的工作。I'm looking for ___ work. "I'm looking for ___ work." 主动告知Share your goal
能加您的微信/LinkedIn 吗？Can I add you on WeChat/LinkedIn? "Could we connect on LinkedIn?" 保持联系Stay in touch
您能介绍我认识 ___ 吗？Could you introduce me to ___? "Could you introduce me to ___?" 请求引荐Ask for an intro
非常感谢您的时间。Thank you for your time. "Thank you so much for your time." 结束时End of conversation
